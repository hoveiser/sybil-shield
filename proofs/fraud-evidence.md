# Fraud report: orion_labs credential claim

This page is a public fraud report filed against the credential claim for the
handle `orion_labs`.

Reported observations:

1. The applicant published a proof page that lists no identity fingerprint.
2. Two other accounts published the same profile biography word for word within
   the same hour.
3. The claim was filed from a wallet funded by the same source as the vouching
   wallet.

The report does not include a signed statement from the account owner and does
not publish a conflicting fingerprint. It is filed as an open challenge so that
the challenger bond is locked in vault custody while validators review the
evidence.

If validators cannot confirm fraud, the bond is returned to the challenger and
the passport stays provisional. If validators confirm fraud, the bond is returned
together with the slashed applicant stake and the slashed voucher stakes.
